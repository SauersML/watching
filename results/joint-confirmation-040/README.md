## Animal errors and a wording limitation

All nine animal errors occur in the `final_line_answer` family: 7/16 answers correct there, and 112/112 across the other seven families. The top tokens on failures are `A` (four), `Ex` (two), `The` (two), and `Yes` (one), not the opposite animal. `errors.json` preserves the exact context IDs, conditions, token IDs, correct-token probabilities, cat/owl mass and source hash.

This family asks for a one-word animal on the **final line**, which leaves earlier prose ambiguous. Strict first-token scoring therefore mixes answer formatting with animal preference in this family. We retain all examples and the original 119/128 score; no post-hoc reinterpretation is counted as a successful answer. No continuation was generated, so eventual answers are unknown. The saved records do not contain the greedy competing token's probability, only its identity; total probability outside cat/owl is reported rather than fabricating a competing-token probability.

This finding is exploratory after inspecting the fixed confirmation. Any training or wording change motivated by it requires a new final test. The v3 bank must not be retrospectively edited or filtered.
